# SOP: Investment & Job Scam Fraud

**SOP ID:** SOP-CYB-005
**Applicable Crime Types:** Fake trading/investment apps, Ponzi-style crypto schemes, work-from-home task scams, fake job offer advance-fee fraud

## 1. Trigger Conditions
Apply this SOP when the complaint mentions:
- Money invested in a trading app/platform promising high returns, now inaccessible
- Payment made for a "job", "task", or "training" that turned out to be fraudulent
- Recruitment via WhatsApp/Telegram group with an unfamiliar company

## 2. Immediate Actions
1. Record all payment trails (UPI/bank/crypto wallet addresses) with timestamps.
2. Preserve the app/platform name, website URL, and any WhatsApp/Telegram group links or admin contacts.
3. Check whether the platform/company is registered with SEBI/RBI (for investment claims) — unregistered status strengthens the fraud case.

## 3. Investigation Path
1. **App/platform trace**: Request app store or hosting provider for developer registration details.
2. **Payment trail**: Follow SOP-CYB-001 for bank/UPI mule account tracing; extend to crypto exchange KYC requests if a wallet address is involved.
3. **Messaging platform request**: Request WhatsApp/Telegram for account registration number and group admin details (via applicable legal process for the platform's jurisdiction).
4. **Group/network mapping**: Identify other victims recruited via the same group/scheme to establish organized fraud pattern.

## 4. Relevant Legal Sections
- BNS Section 318 (Cheating), Section 316 (Criminal breach of trust) — where funds were entrusted
- BNS Section 3(5) (common intention) — where multiple accused acted in concert
- SEBI Act / RBI guidelines — regulatory violation reference for unregistered investment schemes
- BSA Section 63/64 — electronic record admissibility

## 5. Escalation
Escalate to the Economic Offences Wing if the scheme shows signs of a large-scale Ponzi structure or cross-state victim base.
