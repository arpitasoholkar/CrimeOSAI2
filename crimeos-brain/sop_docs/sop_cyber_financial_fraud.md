# SOP: Cyber-Enabled Financial Fraud (UPI / Net-Banking / Card Fraud)

**SOP ID:** SOP-CYB-001
**Applicable Crime Types:** UPI fraud, unauthorized net-banking transactions, credit/debit card fraud, OTP fraud, fake loan app fraud

## 1. Trigger Conditions
Apply this SOP when the complaint mentions:
- Unauthorized debit from a bank account or UPI wallet
- Loss of money after sharing OTP / clicking a link / installing an app
- A caller impersonating a bank official, KYC agent, or government officer

## 2. Immediate Actions (within 1 hour of complaint receipt)
1. Record exact transaction time, amount, UTR/reference number, and beneficiary account/UPI ID.
2. Advise complainant to freeze/block the affected account or card immediately if not already done.
3. Raise a request on the **1930 Cyber Fraud Helpline reporting portal** (National Cybercrime Reporting Portal) if not already lodged, to trigger the interbank freeze mechanism.

## 3. Investigation Path
1. **Bank record request**: Send a legal notice to the victim's bank and the beneficiary bank(s) requesting account opening form (KYC), transaction statement, and IP/device logs used for the fraudulent transaction.
2. **Telecom record request**: If OTP/call-based, request CDR (Call Detail Records) and CAF (Customer Application Form) for the number used to contact the victim.
3. **Beneficiary account trace**: Trace the money trail across mule accounts using bank statements; request freeze on the terminal beneficiary account.
4. **App/Platform request**: If a fake loan app or link was used, request registration and hosting details from the app store / hosting provider / domain registrar.
5. **Digital evidence**: Preserve screenshots, SMS, call recordings if available; forward device for forensic imaging if the complainant permits.

## 4. Relevant Legal Sections
- BNS Section 318 (Cheating) and Section 319 (Cheating by personation)
- BNS Section 308 (Extortion) — if threats were used
- BNSS provisions for search, seizure, and production of documents (Sections 94, 105)
- BSA Section 63 / 64 — admissibility of electronic records (previously IT Act Sec 65B equivalent)
- IT Act Section 66C (identity theft), 66D (cheating by personation using computer resource) — continue to apply as special law alongside BNS

## 5. Escalation
Escalate to the Cyber Crime Cell if amount exceeds ₹1,00,000 or if the fraud involves an organized syndicate (multiple mule accounts, cross-state beneficiaries).
