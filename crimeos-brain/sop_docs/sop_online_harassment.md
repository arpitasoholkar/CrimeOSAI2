# SOP: Online Harassment, Cyberstalking & Non-Consensual Image Sharing

**SOP ID:** SOP-CYB-003
**Applicable Crime Types:** Cyberstalking, morphed/obscene images, revenge content, repeated unwanted contact, impersonation on social media

## 1. Trigger Conditions
Apply this SOP when the complaint mentions:
- Repeated unwanted messages/calls from an online contact
- A fake/impersonating social media profile
- Circulation of private, morphed, or intimate images/videos without consent

## 2. Immediate Actions
1. Preserve the offending post/profile URL, screenshots with timestamps, and any usernames/handles.
2. If content is live and causing ongoing harm, initiate an emergency takedown request to the platform under its trust & safety / law-enforcement portal.
3. Assess complainant's immediate safety; involve a Protection Officer / counselor if the complainant is a minor or in acute distress.

## 3. Investigation Path
1. **Platform request**: Send a legal request to the social media platform for account registration details, login IP history, and linked phone/email.
2. **Content preservation request**: Ask the platform to preserve account data under its legal-preservation process pending formal request, so it is not lost before records arrive.
3. **Number/email trace**: If a phone number or email is associated with the account, request CAF/KYC from telecom/email provider.
4. **Device-level evidence**: If complainant has received content directly, image forensic analysis for metadata (EXIF) may assist geolocation of origin.

## 4. Relevant Legal Sections
- BNS Section 351 (Criminal intimidation), Section 78 (Stalking, including cyberstalking)
- BNS Section 77 (Voyeurism), Section 79 (word/gesture intended to insult modesty) — where applicable
- IT Act Section 66E (violation of privacy), Section 67/67A (publishing obscene/sexually explicit material)
- BSA Section 63/64 — electronic record admissibility
- POCSO Act sections apply in parallel if the victim is a minor — mandatory escalation

## 5. Escalation
Mandatory immediate escalation to a superior officer and Cyber Cell if the victim is a minor, or if there is an explicit threat of image circulation ("sextortion" pattern).
